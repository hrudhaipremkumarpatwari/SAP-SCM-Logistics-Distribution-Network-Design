# Network Design Analysis

## Scenario

Deccan Harvest Foods Pvt. Ltd. is modeled as a millet-based food manufacturer with a Hyderabad manufacturing and consolidation point serving Nizamabad, Vijayawada and Bengaluru.

## Network Design Logic

The design evaluates:

1. Regional demand requirements.
2. Location roles and inventory positioning.
3. Transportation lanes and vehicle capacities.
4. Delivery calendars and schedules.
5. Inventory-transfer requirements.
6. Shipment consolidation opportunities.
7. Disruption-response options.

## Baseline

The baseline model uses 12 weekly transportation trips with approximately 78.1% average vehicle utilization.

## Optimized Scenario

The optimized model consolidates compatible shipments and adjusts route planning. Weekly trips reduce from 12 to 10, while modeled utilization improves to approximately 87.2%.

## Decision Principle

The preferred network should balance service level, transportation cost, inventory availability and operational resilience rather than minimizing only one metric.

All values are illustrative simulation data.

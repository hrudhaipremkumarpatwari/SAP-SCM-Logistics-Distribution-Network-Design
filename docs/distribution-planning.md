# Distribution Planning

## SAP-Oriented Planning Steps

1. Define locations for the manufacturing point and distribution requirements.
2. Create products and relevant location-product combinations.
3. Define transportation lanes between source and destination locations.
4. Maintain transportation calendars and lead times.
5. Define sourcing relationships and planning parameters.
6. Review demand by destination.
7. Plan deployment or inventory transfers.
8. Consolidate compatible transportation requirements.
9. Release transportation requirements for execution.
10. Monitor inventory and service KPIs.

## Modeled Locations

- Hyderabad – manufacturing/consolidation origin
- Nizamabad – regional demand location
- Vijayawada – regional demand location
- Bengaluru – regional demand location

## Inventory Transfer Control

Transfers should be triggered by projected inventory requirements, planned demand, lead time and available source inventory. Emergency replenishment is considered when a disruption threatens the target service level.
